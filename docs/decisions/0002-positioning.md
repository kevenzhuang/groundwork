## Context

Decision 0001 positioned Groundwork as "a lab notebook for AI-era research".
The owner judged that framing too narrow. It describes a recording tool, while the goal
is a standardized way of doing research with humans and AI together.

## Options considered

- A: "Research workflows you can trust, hand over, and build on."
- B: "The rules of the road for human–AI research."
- C: "Workflow infrastructure for research."

## Decision

Option A, organized in three layers:

- **Vision:** make research in the age of AI trustworthy, continuous, and accountable.
- **Category:** the open workflow standard and platform for human–AI research.
- **Tagline:** Research workflows you can trust, hand over, and build on.
  可信、可交接、可传承的科研工作流。

"Lab notebook" remains an explanatory analogy in body text only.

All other parts of 0001 stand: the name, the licenses, the public repository, and the first pilot.

## Reasons

- The three verbs map onto the three pillars: verification (trust), capture and handoff
  (hand over), and decision records with decision rights (build on).
- "Standard + platform" claims the large ambition, but makes clear that the platform is built on
  an open standard. That separates Groundwork from generic workspace tools such as Notion and Feishu.

## Consequences

The README, the spec header, the package description, and the CLI help are updated.
